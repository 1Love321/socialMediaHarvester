# YouTube Public Data kept at most 30 days

YouTube's Developer Policies (§III.E.4) forbid storing statistics of non-authorized channels beyond 30 days and require refreshing or deleting their metadata every 30 days, so YouTube Metric Snapshots, Posts and Raw Payloads for plain Handles are purged after 30 days, unlike Meta Platforms. An audited Analytics & Reporting use case (§III.L) would allow up to 36 months; we chose not to apply for now. The limit is a per-Platform retention setting so it can be raised if that approval is ever obtained. Until then, YouTube data is also not aggregated across different owners' channels (e.g. Brand totals) and no derived YouTube metrics (e.g. engagement rate) are computed. See [the decision](https://github.com/rahmed12/socialMediaHarvester/issues/16) and [the terms research](https://github.com/rahmed12/socialMediaHarvester/issues/5).
